# Plans and billing

Personal workspaces offer **Personal Free** ($0/month, 1 GB) and **Personal Pro** ($20/month, 10 GB). Organizations offer **Business Trial** ($0 for one month only, 1 GB) and **Business Pro** ($100/month, 100 GB). All prices are in USD excluding taxes.

Plans include a token allowance for uploads, file viewing and downloads, workflow emails, requests, and tasks. Plan cards show current rates and an example. Unused tokens expire at the end of the period. See [Usage and tokens](usage.md) for allowances and spending rules.

Personal Free and Business Trial retain requests and notifications for one month. Paid plans allow configurable retention.

## Choose a plan

Select the workspace and open **Plans**. Personal users manage their own subscription; organization Owners and Admins manage the organization's subscription. Ordinary organization members cannot perform plan-card management actions.

Use **Choose** on the desired plan. A paid plan activates only after payment is verified. Follow Checkout and return to Plans to check the result. Closing Checkout or returning from it does not by itself confirm successful payment.

For new organizations, choosing a plan creates the workspace. Paid organizations remain restricted until payment succeeds. Business Trial can be used only once per owner.

## Understand plan changes

The current plan card shows its status. During a pending upgrade, the existing plan can stay Active while the target plan shows a pending payment and **Resolve payment**.

A scheduled change appears as **Planned** with its effective date. Use **Cancel** on a scheduled plan card, when offered, to keep the current paid plan renewing before the change takes effect. Wait for billing confirmation before assuming the schedule has cleared.

Switching to Personal Free or Business Trial sets request and notification retention to one month when the plan becomes active. For a scheduled downgrade, your current retention settings remain until the paid period ends. See [Retention and deletion](retention.md).

## Manage billing

Choose **Manage billing** from the current-plan card to open the billing portal. Use it for supported payment-method updates and invoice history. Plan selection and scheduled-change controls remain on the app's Plans page.

## Resolve a failed payment

If billing blocks a workspace, open Plans and use **Resolve payment** or the available billing action. Other workspace pages and plan changes are unavailable while it is suspended. Review the recovery deadline shown there.

!!! warning "Resolve payment before the recovery deadline"
    Failed renewals suspend workspace access immediately. Use the deadline shown on Plans to complete recovery and retain your data. After that deadline, organizations are removed. Personal workspaces lose workflow data and return to Personal Free while retaining identity and profile. See [Retention and deletion](retention.md).
