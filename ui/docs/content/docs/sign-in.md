# Registration and sign-in

Your user account can access Personal and any organizations where you have employee access. Use the same account consistently when following task links.

## Register

1. [Open the app](/app/) and choose the registration option.
2. Enter your email and a password that satisfies the form's requirements.
3. Confirm the password. Review the [Terms](../terms.md) and [Privacy Policy](../privacy.md), then check **I agree to the Terms and acknowledge the Privacy Policy** and submit.
4. If email confirmation is required, open the confirmation message and follow its link.
5. Sign in and complete your [profile](profile.md).

**Sign up** stays disabled until you check the agreement box.

Registration creates your Personal workspace. Organization membership is managed separately through [Employees](employees.md).

## Sign in

Enter your email and password. If authenticator MFA is enabled, complete the additional challenge with an authenticator code or a recovery code. You can also use a registered [passkey](security.md) when supported.

When you arrive from a task or request email, sign in with the account that has access. A link cannot bypass the resource's permissions.

## Confirm your email

If you cannot find the confirmation message, check spam and use the resend confirmation option. Confirm the address for the account you intend to use. If a link has expired or cannot be accepted, choose **Request a new verification email** on the result page. After successful verification, choose **Sign in** to continue.

## Reset a forgotten password

Choose the password reset option on the sign-in screen, enter your email, and follow the reset message to set a new password. The email-sent and password-reset result pages each offer a **Sign in** button. After the reset succeeds, choose **Sign in** to continue. Password reset and email confirmation are separate flows.

For authenticator or passkey access problems, see [MFA and passkeys](security.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
