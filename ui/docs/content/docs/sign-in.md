# Registration and sign-in

Your account belongs to this installation. Use the email address others use when assigning tasks to you.

## Register

1. [Open the app](/app/) and choose **Sign up**.
2. Enter your email, password, and password confirmation.
3. Submit the form. If email confirmation is required, follow the confirmation email's link.
4. Sign in and complete your [profile](profile.md).

The default local Docker setup disables email confirmation and email delivery. An independently operated installation may enable both.

## Sign in

Enter your email and password. If you enabled authenticator MFA, complete the challenge using your authenticator or a recovery code. You can also sign in with a registered [passkey](security.md) on a supported device.

When following a task link, use the account the task was assigned to.

## Confirm your email

Check spam if the confirmation email is missing and use the resend option. If the link expires, choose **Request a new verification email** on the result page. After verification, choose **Sign in**.

## Reset a forgotten password

Choose the password reset option on the sign-in screen and follow the email instructions. After resetting, choose **Sign in**. Email delivery must be enabled for recovery messages to arrive; contact your installation operator if you cannot receive them.

For authenticator and passkey access, see [MFA and passkeys](security.md).
