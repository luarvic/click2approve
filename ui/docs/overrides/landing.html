A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

One proposal. Three people. A clear outcome.

Alex is a freelance designer. Jamie is the client. Taylor handles the accounts and needs a record of the agreement.

01 / CREATE A REQUEST

Alex sends the proposal.

Alex attaches the website proposal, describes the scope, fee, and delivery date, and asks Jamie to sign it using Jamie’s email address.

Jamie has the document and instructions together, ready to review.

How to create a request →
02 / REVIEW AND SIGN

Jamie reviews the proposal and signs.

Jamie signs in to Click2Approve with the email address Alex used and opens the proposal. After reviewing the document and instructions, Jamie checks the legal name, draws a signature, and submits the response.

The signature and decision become part of the request history.

How to complete a signing task →
03 / SHARE AND VERIFY

Taylor checks what was agreed.

When Jamie’s signature completes the request, Click2Approve creates a receipt recording the outcome. Alex opens that receipt and creates a verification link with an expiry date. Taylor follows the link to review the recorded decision, signer, and signature without signing in.

With a copy of the proposal, Taylor can use Verify file to check that its contents match a file recorded on the receipt.

Anyone with an active link can view that receipt. Alex can remove the link to stop sharing.
